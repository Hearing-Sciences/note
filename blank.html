Welcome to Hearing Sciences at the University of Nottingham

Get involved in hearing research

This website is used to conduct research into hearing and related conditions such as tinnitus, hyperacusis, and balance disorders. Here you can find research projects being run by Hearing Sciences, part of the School of Medicine at the University of Nottingham, and as part of the Hearing Theme of the NIHR Nottingham Biomedical Research Centre. For additional information regarding our hearing research, please email adele.horobin@nottingham.ac.uk or follow us on Twitter: @uonhearsci and @hearingnihr
